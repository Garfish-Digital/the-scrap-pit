The Scrap Pit is being reworked as part of a broader Garfish Digital demo site refresh.

**Key decisions (confirmed 2026-03-28)**:
- Convert from multi-page (4 pages) to single long-scroll page
- Retain: brutalist design, chromatic displacement effects, duotone/mono-to-color filters, grit cursor
- Light theme stays (rgb(248, 248, 255)), not going dark
- Keep ALL existing sections except individual page heroes (About/Contact/Pricing heroes become section headers within the scroll)
- "Join the Fight", "Ready to be Forged?", and "30-Day Warrior Guarantee" are all KEPT
- Dark/light rhythm: Dark hero → Light content → Light2 (gradient) → Dark header → Light content → repeat
- Fighters section changes from bg-dark to light
- 30-Day Guarantee moves to light (was dark)
- Section headers get staggered punchy entrance animations (title, subtitle, description at different times/speeds)
- Training blocks get high-action hover/tap interactivity with maintained color displacement
- Nav keeps current look, becomes smooth-scroll anchors with punchy underline hover animation
- Footer stays, links become scroll anchors
- Quick action buttons in Contact become smooth-scroll links
- Site is embedded in garfishdigital.com, not standalone
- Roadmap written to SITE_REWORK.md for Sonnet to execute
- Copy will be reworked separately after restructure

**Why:** Garfish reworked their main site and learned lessons over the past year. Demos are being reduced from 8 to 4 and restructured as embeds. Quality demos that capture the dark luxury aesthetic are the top priority.

**How to apply:** Prioritize visual impact and brand alignment over feature count. Plan carefully before executing. Opus plans, Sonnet builds. When in doubt about removing content, keep it — can always remove later.
