Rob is the owner/operator of Garfish Digital (garfishdigital.com), a web development company.

**Target market**: Darker, edgier businesses and organizations — tattoo shops, heavy metal bands, pagan suppliers/communities, combat sports, motorcycle culture.

**Brand aesthetic**: Dark luxury. Of 4 remaining demo sites, The Scrap Pit is the only light-themed one.

**Business model**: Build sites that look like $10,000 but price them $2,000-$3,000 to be affordable for their target market.

**Technical stack**: Astro-based demos, SCSS styling. Originally had 8 linked demos, now keeping 4 structured as embeds on the main garfishdigital.com site.

**Workflow preference**: Uses Opus as the creative/strategic planner, Sonnet as the executor. Iterative process — Opus creates roadmap documents, Sonnet follows them. Quality over speed.
